The proposal

A small, time-boxed pilot that is easy to reverse

Who
12

Support team members move to a four-day week. Everyone else stays on the current schedule.

How long
3 mo

Long enough to see past the novelty effect, short enough to stop cleanly if the numbers slip.

Coverage
5 days

Days off are staggered across the group, so customers see the same support hours they see today.

How we will know

Two customer metrics decide whether it worked

Metric 1

CSAT

Are customers as satisfied with the support they get from the pilot group?

Pass: at or above the pre-pilot baseline
Metric 2

Ticket resolution time

Are tickets being closed as quickly with four working days per agent?

Pass: at or below the pre-pilot baseline
